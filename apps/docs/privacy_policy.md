# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Introduction

MediAI ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and complying with data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our ICU Risk Prediction Platform.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Data
We collect information that you provide directly to us:

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, username, password
- **Professional Information**: Role, medical license number (if applicable), organization
- **Authentication Data**: Login credentials, session tokens

### Protected Health Information (PHI)
When using the prediction features, we process:

- **Patient Demographics**: Age, gender, BMI
- **Vital Signs**: Heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, respiratory rate, SpO2
- **Laboratory Results**: WBC count, lactate, creatinine, platelets, etc.
- **Clinical Scores**: SOFA scores, APACHE-II scores
- **Temporal Data**: ICU admission time, length of stay

### Technical Data
We automatically collect:

- **Usage Data**: Pages visited, features used, time spent
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system
- **Performance Data**: Response times, error logs

## 2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

- **Consent**: You have given explicit consent for processing (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
- **Contract**: Processing is necessary for contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
- **Legal Obligation**: Compliance with legal requirements (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)
- **Legitimate Interests**: For security, fraud prevention, and system improvement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

For special categories of data (health data), we rely on:
- **Explicit Consent** (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR)
- **Healthcare Provision** (Art. 9(2)(h) GDPR)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

### Primary Purposes
- **Risk Prediction**: Generate sepsis and mortality risk predictions
- **Clinical Decision Support**: Provide AI-powered insights
- **Account Management**: Maintain user accounts and authentication
- **Platform Functionality**: Enable core features and services

### Secondary Purposes
- **Security**: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access
- **Compliance**: Meet HIPAA, GDPR, and other legal obligations
- **Analytics**: Improve model accuracy and platform performance
- **Audit**: Maintain comprehensive audit trails

## 4. Data Protection Measures

### Technical Safeguards
- **Encryption**: AES-256 encryption for data at rest
- **Transport Security**: TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access control (RBAC)
- **Authentication**: Multi-factor authentication (MFA) support
- **Pseudonymization**: De-identification of PHI where possible

### Organizational Safeguards
- **Staff Training**: Regular HIPAA/GDPR training for all personnel
- **Access Logging**: Comprehensive audit trails of all PHI access
- **Incident Response**: Documented breach notification procedures
- **Data Protection Impact Assessments**: Regular privacy risk assessments

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your information for:

- **Account Data**: Until account deletion + 30 days
- **PHI**: 7 years from last access (HIPAA requirement)
- **Audit Logs**: 7 years (regulatory compliance)
- **Anonymized Analytics**: Indefinitely (no personal identification)

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Share Your Data Except:

- **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly authorize sharing
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or court order
- **Service Providers**: Third parties under strict data processing agreements
- **Emergency**: To prevent harm or protect vital interests

### We NEVER:
- Sell your personal data
- Use PHI for marketing
- Share data with unauthorized third parties

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the following rights:

### Right to Access (Art. 15)
Request a copy of your personal data we hold.

### Right to Rectification (Art. 16)
Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

### Right to Erasure (Art. 17)
Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten").

### Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18)
Limit how we process your data.

### Right to Data Portability (Art. 20)
Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

### Right to Object (Art. 21)
Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

### Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))
Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

### Right to Lodge a Complaint
File a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

**To Exercise Your Rights**: Contact privacy@mediai.example.com

## 8. HIPAA Privacy Rights

If you are a patient whose PHI is processed:

- **Right to Access**: Obtain a copy of your health records
- **Right to Amend**: Request corrections to your health information
- **Right to Accounting**: Receive a list of PHI disclosures
- **Right to Restrict**: Request restrictions on PHI use/disclosure
- **Right to Confidential Communications**: Request confidential communication methods
- **Right to Breach Notification**: Be notified of PHI breaches

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction. We ensure adequate safeguards through:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses** (EU Commission approved)
- **Adequacy Decisions** (for countries with adequate protection)
- **Binding Corporate Rules** (where applicable)

## 10. Children's Privacy

MediAI is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 11. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for:
- **Session Management**: Maintain user login state
- **Security**: Prevent CSRF attacks
- **Performance**: Monitor system health

You can control cookies through browser settings.

## 12. Data Breach Notification

In case of a personal data breach, we will:

- **Notify Supervisory Authority**: Within 72 hours (GDPR requirement)
- **Notify Affected Individuals**: Without undue delay if high risk
- **Document Breach**: Maintain breach register (GDPR Art. 33)
- **Notify HHS**: Within required timeframes (HIPAA requirement)
